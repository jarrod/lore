# Governance

Lore is currently maintained by [@jarrod](https://github.com/jarrod).

Contributors are encouraged to participate through issues, discussions, pull requests, and review. Maintainers seek rough consensus for design changes while retaining responsibility for repository direction, compatibility, security response, releases, and final merge decisions.

Maintainer status may be extended to sustained contributors who demonstrate sound technical judgment, constructive collaboration, and reliable stewardship of Lore's compatibility and safety invariants. Governance changes are made publicly through pull requests.
